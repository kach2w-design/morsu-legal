# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 26 August 2026**

Morsu is a cook-along app. It shows you a recipe one step at a time, with a
picture and a voice reading each step aloud, so you can cook without stopping to
read.

This policy explains what we hold, why, and what you can do about it. It was
written by going through what the app actually stores rather than from a
template.

Morsu is operated by **Melvin Chau**, 210 Gaven Street, San Francisco, CA 94134,
United States. For anything in this policy, write to **contactmorsu@gmail.com**.

---

## The short version

- You need an account, so we hold your email address.
- We store what you save and what you cook.
- If you photograph something you cooked, we keep that photo.
- **There is no advertising in Morsu and no third-party tracking of any
  kind.** No ad networks, no analytics companies, nobody watching you but us.
- We do not sell your data. There is nobody to sell it to.
- Delete your account in Settings and all of the above goes with it.

## What we hold

### Things you give us

**Your email address**, so you can sign in and recover your account. If you sign
in with Google or Apple we receive your email from them and never see a
password.

**A username, a display name, and which chef avatar you picked.**

**Photographs of food you have cooked**, if you take them, along with any note
or rating you leave on that cook.

### Things the app records as you use it

**Your cooking history** — which recipes you started, which step you reached,
whether you finished, and roughly how long it took. This is what lets you pick a
recipe back up where you left off.

**Progress** — experience points and achievements.

**Product events**, like "cook started" or "recipe saved", written to our own
database so we can see which parts of the app work and which do not. These are
tied to your account.

**Crash and error diagnostics**, when something in the app fails: the type of
error, a message with personal details stripped out, and the technical trace
showing where it happened. These are tied to your account, and they are **on by
default**. You can turn them off at any time in Settings, under Diagnostics, and
nothing further is sent from that moment.

We hold these for the same reason as product events: an error nobody reports is
an error nobody fixes. They are never sold, never shared, and never used to
advertise to you.

> This is first-party only. There is **no Google Analytics, no Firebase
> Analytics, no advertising SDK and no third-party tracker anywhere in Morsu.**
> Our own database is the only place any of it goes.

### Things we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, calendar, or any health data. We do
not track you
across other apps or websites, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do
not sell or rent personal information.

**Your date of birth is never stored.** Morsu asks your age once, at sign-up, to
confirm you are 13 or older. The answer is used at that moment and discarded.

**The microphone** is used only for the in-app voice commands — "Chef, next
step" — only while a cook-along is open, and only if you grant permission.

Your speech is sent to Apple's speech recognition service to be turned into
text, so that the app can tell "Chef, next step" from anything else you say in
your kitchen. Apple handles that recognition under its own privacy policy.

**Morsu never records or stores audio.** We receive the recognised words, act on
the ones that are commands, and keep nothing.

## Why we hold it

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Email, password | So you can sign in, and recover the account if you forget |
| Username, display name, avatar | So the app knows what to call you |
| Saved recipes | So they are there next time |
| Cooking history | To resume a recipe, and to show what you have made |
| Photos and notes | Because you chose to keep them |
| Product events | To find which parts of the app are failing people |
| Crash diagnostics | To find and fix what breaks, without waiting for someone to report it |

Photographs are optional, and we rely on your choosing to use them. The rest is
what running the service requires.

## Who else sees it

**Supabase** hosts our database and handles sign-in. **Cloudflare** serves the
recipe pictures and the narration audio. **Google and Apple** are involved only
if you use them to sign in, or when you subscribe.

That is the entire list. Nobody else receives your information, and nobody pays
us for access to it.

We would disclose information if the law genuinely required it, and we would
tell you whenever we are permitted to.

---

## Subscriptions

Morsu Premium is billed by Apple, not by us. **We never see your card details**
— Apple handles payment, tells us whether your subscription is active, and pays
us. What we store is which plan you are on and when it renews or expires.

If you start a free trial and do not cancel before it ends, Apple charges you
and the subscription continues. You can cancel any time in your device's
subscription settings, and you keep access until the period you have paid for
runs out.

---

## How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists.

**Delete your account at Settings → Danger zone → Delete account.** That removes
your profile, your saved recipes, your cooking history, your photographs, your
progress, and every report you have filed.

Deletion is permanent and we cannot undo it. Backups are overwritten on a
rolling basis and are fully cleared within 30 days.

## Your choices

**See what we hold.** Ask at **contactmorsu@gmail.com** and we will send it to
you.

**Correct it.** Most of it is editable in the app. Anything else, write to us.

**Delete it.** Settings → Danger zone → Delete account.

**Turn things off.** Photographs are optional. Microphone permission can be revoked in your phone's settings and the app
carries on working without it — the on-screen controls do everything the voice
commands do.

If you are in the UK, the EU, California or another place with data protection
law, you may also have rights to object, to restrict how we use your
information, to receive a copy in a portable form, and to complain to your
regulator. Write to us and we will help. We do not charge for this and we will
not treat you differently for asking.

## Children

**Morsu is for people aged 13 and over.** You are asked to confirm your age when
you create an account, and accounts are not created for anyone younger.

Morsu is deliberately built to be followed without reading, and children can and
do cook. We think that is a good thing — with an adult. **A younger child should
be cooking alongside a parent or guardian, on the adult's account.** Hobs,
ovens, knives and hot oil are not things to leave a child alone with, whatever
an app makes easy.

If we learn that we hold information from a child under 13 we delete it. If you
believe your child has created an account, write to **contactmorsu@gmail.com**
and we will remove it.

## Security

Passwords are hashed by Supabase and are never visible to us. Everything travels
encrypted. Database access is restricted per user by row-level security, so your
records can only be reached by you — this is enforced by the database itself
rather than by the app asking nicely.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you we will tell you, and the
relevant regulator, within the time the law requires.

## Where your information lives

Our database and file storage are hosted in the United States. If you are
outside the United States, your information is transferred there and held under
the transfer protections our providers offer.
