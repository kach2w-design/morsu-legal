# Morsu Support

**Last updated: 28 August 2026**

Morsu is a cook-along app: recipes read aloud step by step, with voice control
and timers for when your hands are busy. This page is how you reach us, and the
answers to the questions we expect most.

## Contact

Write to **contactmorsu@gmail.com**.

We aim to reply within **2 business days**. Account-deletion and data requests
are handled with priority — see below.

When you write, it helps to include: the email address on your Morsu account,
your iPhone model, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do the voice commands work?

While a cook is open, address the app as **"Chef"** followed by a command:

- **"Chef next"** — next step (also "Chef next step", "Chef continue")
- **"Chef back"** — previous step
- **"Chef repeat"** — hear the step again
- **"Chef start timer"** / **"Chef stop timer"**
- **"Chef finish"** — end the cook
- **"Chef stop talking"** — quiet the narration

Saying "Chef" alone does nothing — a command only fires when an action word
follows, so ordinary kitchen talk does not move your recipe. Every command also
has an on-screen button, so you can cook entirely without voice if you prefer.

### Is Morsu listening to me? Is anything recorded?

The microphone is on only while a cook is open. Nothing is recorded or saved by
Morsu. Speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition, used only to
match the fixed commands above, and never stored by us or shared with anyone.
You can decline the microphone permission and use the buttons instead.

### How does the free trial and subscription work?

Morsu+ starts with **7 days free** on every plan — Weekly, Monthly and Annual
are billing schedules, not different offers. After the trial, payment is
charged to your Apple Account, and the subscription renews automatically unless
you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or
cancel any time in your iPhone's **Settings → your name → Subscriptions**, or
from the Subscription row inside Morsu's Settings. Any unused portion of a free
trial is forfeited when a subscription is purchased.

### I paid but the app doesn't show my subscription

Tap **Restore Purchases** on the Morsu+ screen. If that does not fix it, email
us with the address on your account and we will sort it out.

### How do ranks and XP work?

Finishing a cook earns XP — more the first time you cook a recipe, and a quick
tap-through does not count as a cook. The ladder runs Bronze, Silver, Platinum,
Diamond, Emerald, Master Chef. Your rank is calculated on our server, so it is
the same on any phone you sign in on. The full ladder is in the app under
**How ranks work**.

### How do I delete my account?

In the app: **Settings → Delete account**. Everything we hold about you goes
with it. Backups clear on a rolling basis within 30 days. If you cannot get
into the app, email us from the address on the account and we will do it for
you.

### Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes. Email **contactmorsu@gmail.com** from the address on your account and we
will send you what we hold.

### Does Morsu handle allergies?

No. Morsu does not filter for allergens and makes no allergy claims. Always
check ingredients yourself against your own dietary needs — see our
[Terms of Service](https://kach2w-design.github.io/morsu-legal/TERMS_OF_SERVICE),
section "Cooking safely".

### I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, choose the email option and tap **Forgot your password?** (the control's shipped label).
The reset link arrives by email.

### How old do I have to be?

Morsu accounts are for people **13 and older**. Under 18, you need a parent or
guardian's permission.
